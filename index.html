About me

who I am

I’m Menna, a creative content creator and social media designer with experience in e-commerce and digital marketing. I create engaging content, AI-powered visuals, and short-form videos that help brands stand out and connect with their audiences. I enjoy bringing ideas to life through visual storytelling, thoughtful content, and platform-specific strategies. From managing social media accounts to optimizing product listings, I combine creativity with a practical understanding of what helps a brand grow.
